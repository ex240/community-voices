Community Voices — Hacker News

This app produces a weekly Community Voices document: what Hacker News discussed in the past seven days, and a clearly labeled prediction of what it may discuss next week. Reports are grounded in retrieved comments (RAG) and compared with a no-RAG baseline that receives no current-week evidence.

This is a bounded seven-day sample, not an archive of all Hacker News. Next-week items are predictions, not observations.
